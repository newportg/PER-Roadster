# Smart Roadster Coupé BRABUS build BOM

## Purpose

This is a donor-based build checklist for a **factory-style BRABUS Roadster
Coupé**. It is intended to answer “what am I missing?” before purchasing
parts. It is not yet a final ordering list because exact part numbers,
supersessions and option differences require the donor VIN and an EPC.

The safest and most economical route is normally:

1. Buy the most complete, structurally sound 452 Coupé donor available.
2. Prefer a complete factory BRABUS Coupé donor if authenticity and total cost
   matter.
3. If using a standard Coupé, retain every donor part until the car is running
   and electrically complete.
4. Source BRABUS-specific parts as a second, separately tracked package.
5. Photograph, label and bag every removed fastener and connector.

## Build definition

The target needs an explicit definition before parts are ordered:

| Decision | Factory-style target |
|---|---|
| Body | 452 Roadster Coupé with glass/targa roof |
| Power | BRABUS 74 kW / approximately 101 PS calibration |
| Transmission | Six-speed single-clutch automated manual |
| Exterior | BRABUS front spoiler, side skirts, grille and associated trim |
| Chassis | BRABUS suspension specification |
| Wheels | BRABUS 17-inch staggered wheel package |
| Exhaust | BRABUS twin sports exhaust and related heat shielding |
| Interior | BRABUS trim, instruments and branded details where fitted |
| Electronics | VIN-compatible ECU/SAM/TCU/immobiliser system |

Some special editions and markets combined equipment differently. “BRABUS
style” is not the same as a factory BRABUS vehicle; record which definition is
being built.

## Acquisition priority

### Donor car: must-have before purchase

- [ ] Complete 452 Coupé body shell and Tridion safety cell
- [ ] VIN/data card and clear ownership record
- [ ] Engine bay, rear subframe and suspension pickup points free of structural
      corrosion or impact distortion
- [ ] Complete engine, turbo, intercooler and cooling system
- [ ] Complete gearbox, clutch and both automated actuators
- [ ] ECU, TCU, SAM, ABS/ESP, SRS, cluster, keys and immobiliser components
- [ ] Complete engine and body wiring harnesses
- [ ] Both roof panels, roof seals, latches and storage hardware
- [ ] All glass, doors, lights, body panels and interior trim
- [ ] Wheels or at least the correct wheel/tyre specification evidence
- [ ] Complete brake and suspension hardware
- [ ] Service records and scan report, if available

### Donor rejection conditions

- [ ] Severely corroded Tridion or suspension mounting structure
- [ ] Missing VIN/data card or uncertain ownership
- [ ] Flooded cabin/SAM with extensive unidentified wiring corrosion
- [ ] Major fire damage or cut/repaired primary harness
- [ ] Missing engine, gearbox and control modules unless a second donor exists
- [ ] Coupé roof structure missing when a factory Coupé is required
- [ ] Parts removed without labels, photographs or fastener segregation

## Parts that a single donor often does not provide

- New roof, glass and weather seals
- New clutch, brake discs/pads and tyres
- New water pump, hoses and coolant pipes
- New turbo oil lines and gaskets
- New suspension bushes, dampers and springs
- New wheel bearings and driveshaft boots
- New exhaust fasteners and heat shields
- New battery, fluids, filters and plugs
- Correct BRABUS wheels and tyres
- Replacement modules after water damage
- Coding, teach-in and calibration services
- Missing clips, brackets and one-time-use fasteners

## Completion gates

Do not call the build complete until the following are documented:

1. VIN and option data recorded.
2. Every catalog row has a status: installed, purchased, missing, not fitted or
   rejected.
3. Engine, gearbox, ECU, TCU, SAM, ABS/ESP and SRS identities are recorded.
4. No water remains in the cabin or module areas.
5. Cooling, fuel pressure, charging and brake hydraulic tests pass.
6. Gearbox clutch/gear teach-in completes without persistent faults.
7. Four-wheel alignment is measured and recorded.
8. All safety systems scan without unresolved faults.
9. Roadworthiness, emissions, wheel/tyre and lighting requirements are checked
   for the registration country.
10. A controlled road test is completed only after static safety checks pass.

## Shared versus BRABUS-specific matrix

This matrix is a procurement guide, not a guarantee of interchangeability.
“Shared” means the standard Coupé donor is the expected source. “Option-check”
means the part may be shared but must be matched by VIN, body style, market,
equipment or revision. “BRABUS” means do not assume the standard part produces
the factory specification.

### Normally shared with a standard Roadster Coupé

| Area | Shared parts to retain from Coupé donor | Important checks |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Tridion cell, subframes, crash structures, mounting points | Structural condition and repair history |
| Body | Doors, hinges, latches, glass, windscreen, Coupé roof shell | Body style and left/right-hand drive |
| Roof | Glass roof panels, latches, storage hardware and seals | Panel revision and seal condition |
| Electrical | Main body harness, door harnesses, lighting harnesses | Options, market and water damage |
| Controls | Instrument cluster, switches, steering column hardware | Coding and equipment level |
| Safety | Airbags, belts, SRS sensors and mounting hardware | Exact module and pretensioner match |
| HVAC | Heater box, blower, controls and ducting | Air-conditioning option |
| Cooling | Radiator, fan, coolant pipes and expansion system | Engine/AC configuration |
| Fuel | Tank, filler, sender and most fuel plumbing | Pump, pressure and emissions revision |
| Transmission casing | Six-speed gearbox casing and basic gearset | Gearbox identification and internal condition |
| Driveline | Driveshafts, hubs and differential interfaces | Spline, ABS and gearbox code |
| Steering | Rack, column, tie rods and steering hardware | LHD/RHD and alignment |
| Brake base system | Lines, master cylinder and many standard brake components | Caliper/disc package and ABS/ESP |

## Procurement rule

For each component, place it in one of four bins:

1. **Donor shared:** keep the standard Coupé part unless damaged.
2. **Donor shared, verify:** keep it, but do not order a replacement until the
   VIN, option code, connector or measurement is recorded.
3. **BRABUS source:** obtain the correct factory BRABUS component or an
   explicitly documented equivalent.
4. **New service item:** replace regardless of donor condition when age, safety,
   sealing or wear makes reuse unreliable.

Do not dismantle or sell a standard Coupé donor's apparently ordinary parts
until the BRABUS conversion is complete. Many “shared” pieces are difficult to
source after the donor is stripped, especially brackets, harness branches,
seals, roof hardware, clips and module connectors.

## Availability risk: parts to secure first

There is no public, VIN-independent master list of every Mercedes/smart part
that has reached “no longer available” status. Availability varies by market,
dealer stock, supersession, breakers and specialist inventory. The table below
is therefore a **procurement risk register**, not a declaration that every
listed part is permanently unobtainable.

| Risk | Parts likely to be difficult or unavailable new | Best strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Very high | BRABUS front spoiler, side skirts, grille and original mounting hardware | Buy complete with donor; preserve damaged originals for repair/templates |
| Very high | BRABUS twin sports exhaust, heat shields and brackets | Secure a complete system; inspect for cracks and corrosion before purchase |
| Very high | BRABUS Monoblock wheels, correct caps and undamaged originals | Buy only after checking casting, offset, cracks, buckling and repairs |
| Very high | BRABUS interior trim, seats, pedals, gear knob and instrument details | Source as a complete interior or buy used from a specialist |
| Very high | Coupé glass roof panels, latches, storage hardware and moulded seals | Keep every roof component from the donor; inspect for chips and distortion |
| High | SAM/body power module and compatible coded electronics | Preserve the complete module/key/ECU set; water damage makes used units risky |
| High | Engine ECU, TCU, immobiliser/key components and instrument cluster | Keep matched modules together; record software and coding before removal |
| High | Main, engine, door and roof harness branches | Retain every harness and connector; repair rather than substitute generically |
| High | Roof seals, mirror seals, roof-peak seals and unusual weatherstrips | Buy new when available; otherwise preserve patterns and use specialist seals |
| High | BRABUS springs, dampers and non-standard suspension hardware | Buy a complete matched set; do not mix unknown used spring rates |
| High | Model-specific body clips, brackets, undertrays and heat shields | Bag and label every donor fastener and bracket |
| Medium-high | Front/rear lights, trim lenses, switches, gauges and small interior plastics | Buy sound used parts before dismantling the donor |
| Medium-high | Fuel tank, sender and pump variants | Keep the complete donor assembly; verify emissions and connector revision |
| Medium | Coolant pipes, formed hoses and special clamps | Replace corroded metal pipes; preserve good originals as patterns |
| Medium | Gearbox actuators and hydraulic components | Reconditionable in many cases; retain cores and record actuator versions |
| Medium | Turbocharger and wastegate hardware | Rebuild/remanufacture is possible; retain identification plate and brackets |
| Lower | Filters, plugs, belts, bearings, bushes, brake consumables and fluids | Normally available aftermarket, but verify exact application |

### Practical meaning of “unobtainable”

- **NLA-new:** no normal manufacturer/dealer supply is currently found.
- **Used-only:** breakers, donor cars, enthusiast stock or private sale are the
  realistic source.
- **Remanufacturable:** the part can often be rebuilt, cloned or repaired, but
  the core must be retained.
- **Reproducible:** a specialist can fabricate or reproduce it, subject to
  correct material, geometry and safety requirements.
- **Available aftermarket:** a replacement exists, but it may not match the
  factory appearance, calibration or approval.

### Parts to remove and store before the donor moves

1. All BRABUS exterior parts and their brackets.
2. Both Coupé roof panels, seals, latches and storage hardware.
3. ECU, TCU, SAM, ABS/ESP, SRS, cluster, keys and security components.
4. Engine, body, roof and door harnesses with every connector.
5. Exhaust, heat shields, turbo plumbing and mounting hardware.
6. BRABUS wheels, caps and tyre specification labels.
7. BRABUS seats, interior trim, gauges, pedals and gear controls.
8. All unusual clips, brackets, undertrays, seals and fasteners.
