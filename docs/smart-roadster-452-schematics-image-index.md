# Smart Roadster 452 schematics and image index

## Yes, technical images exist

The public 452 figure index currently identifies **78 illustrations,
schematics, exploded views and procedure photographs**:

- 55 body/interior
- 17 electrical
- 4 chassis
- 1 HVAC/comfort
- 1 powertrain

These are source-linked figures, not a complete replacement for the factory
workshop manual. Use them to identify a component, diagram page or repair area,
then verify the exact part and procedure against the donor VIN.

## Original project diagrams

The [reference dossier](./smart-roadster-452-reference-dossier.md) contains
original, non-OEM Mermaid diagrams for:

- Mechanical powertrain flow
- Electrical module/CAN architecture

These diagrams are intentionally functional rather than pin-level. They should
not be used as substitutes for the OEM connector and wire-colour schematics.

## Copyright and source handling

The linked pages host or index manual-derived images. This project links to
those resources and records what they contain rather than copying complete
manual pages into the repository. A lawfully obtained manual can be used to
populate internal part references, measurements and donor photographs.
