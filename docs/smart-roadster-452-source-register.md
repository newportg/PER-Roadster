# Smart Roadster 452 source and evidence register

## Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Official manufacturer data, VIN record or legally obtained workshop information | Exact service, fitment or safety work after matching vehicle scope |
| B | Independent catalogue or multiple consistent public technical sources | Planning and cross-checking; verify before repair |
| C | Enthusiast guide, forum, tuning page or uncited special-edition claim | Hypothesis, inspection lead or owner experience only |

## Public-source register

### 1. Smart Roadster overview

Source: [Wikipedia Smart Roadster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_Roadster)

Useful for:

- W452 identity, body styles and rear-mid/rear-wheel-drive layout
- Approximate production period and production quantity
- Headline dimensions, wheelbase and mass
- Standard/high-output engine description
- BRABUS body, wheel and power differences
- Historical special editions and prototype context

Limitations:

- The page identifies its own citation gaps.
- Some special-edition and production claims are not independently sourced.
- Use as orientation and cross-reference, not as the final repair authority.

Class: **B/C depending on the individual claim**.

### 2. M160 engine family

Source: [Mercedes-Benz M160 engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes-Benz_M160_engine)

Useful for:

- 0.6 L and 0.7 L family distinction
- 698 cc displacement
- Bore and stroke: 66.5 mm and 67 mm
- SOHC, two valves per cylinder, turbocharging, fuel injection and water cooling
- Published output/torque range
- Association of E07 variants with the Roadster and BRABUS

Limitations:

- Engine-family data includes models other than the 452.
- Exact engine code, calibration, emissions equipment and software remain
  vehicle-specific.

Class: **B** for family data; **A required** for a particular engine.

### 3. Parkers catalogue data

Sources:

- [Roadster Coupé range](https://www.parkers.co.uk/smart/roadster/coupe-2003/specs/)
- [80 bhp Coupé LHD](https://www.parkers.co.uk/smart/roadster/coupe-2003/(80bhp)-2d-auto-(lhd)/specs/)

Useful for:

- Catalogue dimensions and wheelbase
- 35 L fuel capacity
- Approximately 810 kg listed Coupé mass
- 698 cc, three cylinders, six-speed transmission and rear-wheel drive
- Catalogue performance for the 80 bhp version
- Public equipment examples: ABS/EBD, ESP/HBA, airbags, roof equipment and
  wheel options
- Indexed BRABUS, Racing Edition and Finale variants

Limitations:

- Trim equipment can vary over the production run.
- “Automatic” is catalogue terminology; the mechanism is an automated manual.
- Catalogue figures are not a condition assessment or proof of VIN fitment.

Class: **B**.

### 4. Smart Car Manuals 452 index

Source: [Smart Car Manuals 452 model index](https://smartcarmanuals.com/models/452-roadster/)

Useful for:

- A public index of owner, service and technical references
- 452 engine and transmission orientation
- Water-ingress, SAM, turbo and gearbox-adaptation investigation leads
- Links to public manual/diagram indexes
- Model/variant vocabulary for future parts records

Limitations:

- Community-curated content and third-party hosting.
- The page contains catalogue and owner-practice statements alongside manual
  indexes; they must not be treated as equally authoritative.
- Exact values and procedures require the named source manual.

Class: **B/C**.

### 5. Fluids and specification quick reference

Source: [Smart Car Manuals fluids reference](https://smartcarmanuals.com/maintenance/fluids-spec-table/)

Useful planning values:

- 452 engine oil: approximately 5W-40 ACEA A3/B4 and about 3.0 L with filter
- 452 coolant: approximately 4–5 L
- Brake fluid: DOT 4 / MB 331.0 family
- Common cold tyre-pressure range: lower front and higher rear pressure
- Publicly listed 452 wheel-bolt torque planning value

Limitations:

- The page explicitly says to verify the final specification in the workshop
  manual and against the vehicle.
- Approximate capacity is not a fill instruction.
- The page mixes chassis generations; select only the 452 row.

Class: **B for planning; A required before service**.

### 6. Gear-ratio figure index

Source: [Smart Car Manuals gear-ratio figure](https://smartcarmanuals.com/figures/f3ff3f50c-gear-ratios-table-for-fortwo-and-roadster-smart-models/)

Useful for:

- Locating a published ratio table for the Smart transmission family
- Establishing that exact ratio data should be captured from the figure/manual,
  not guessed from a generic six-speed listing

Limitations:

- The public page is an index to an image/manual figure.
- Exact ratios must be transcribed from the applicable transmission and variant.

Class: **B**, pending VIN/transmission confirmation.

### 7. Maintenance schedule reference

Source: [Smart Car Manuals maintenance schedule](https://smartcarmanuals.com/maintenance/maintenance-schedule/)

Useful for:

- Age-based inspection priorities
- Brake-fluid and coolant time considerations
- General filter, plug, belt and brake inspection planning
- Reminder that age-related rubber, hoses, seals and corrosion are important
  on an old 452

Limitations:

- The page is primarily organized around other Smart generations.
- It is not a 452 factory service schedule.
- Use it to build questions, not to close a service record.

Class: **C/B for planning only**.

### 8. Smart Roadster Tech suspension guide

Source: [Suspension setup guide](https://smartroadster.tech/smart-roadster-suspension-setup-guide/)

Useful for:

- Owner/enthusiast setup topics: alignment, bushes, dampers, ride height,
  tyre fitment and symptoms
- Identifying measurements that should be verified on an alignment rack

Limitations:

- The page is not manufacturer documentation.
- Its suspension description and numerical alignment targets should not be
  promoted to factory data without primary confirmation.
- Modified-car recommendations are not necessarily safe for a standard car.

Class: **C**.

### 9. Smart Roadster Tech gearbox guide

Source: [Gearbox adaptation guide](https://smartroadster.tech/smart-roadster-gearbox-adaptation-reset/)

Useful for:

- Explaining the single-clutch automated-manual architecture
- Separating clutch actuation from gear selection
- Identifying useful scan data and symptoms
- Explaining why adaptation cannot repair a mechanical fault

Limitations:

- The exact reset/teach-in sequence is tool- and software-dependent.
- Use the diagnostic system's vehicle-specific procedure.
- Never experiment with adaptation on a vehicle that is unsafe to move.

Class: **C** for procedure; **B** for general architecture.

## Consolidated values requiring confirmation

| Topic | Current public baseline | Confirmation required |
|---|---|---|
| Production years | Historical sources: 2002–2005; catalogues: 2003–2006 | VIN/build date and market |
| Standard engine output | About 60–62 hp or 80–82 hp depending on calibration | ECU/data card |
| BRABUS output | About 99–101 hp | Engine/ECU/option evidence |
| Coupé mass | About 810–815 kg | Equipment and weighbridge |
| Engine oil | About 3.0 L, 5W-40 ACEA A3/B4 planning value | Exact engine and workshop source |
| Coolant | About 4–5 L planning value | Drain/refill procedure and exact circuit |
| Tyres | Commonly 175/55 R15 front, 195/50 R15 rear | Wheel stamp, tyre label and approval |
| Gear ratios | Published figure exists | Transmission/variant-specific figure |
| Wiring | Module/CAN architecture is known | Official schematic for pin-level work |

## Local evidence to collect

For each project vehicle, add one record or photo per item:

- VIN plate and dashboard VIN
- Vehicle data card and option codes
- Engine and gearbox identification
- ECU/TCU/SAM/ABS/SRS scan reports
- Wheel and tyre markings
- Fluid labels and service invoices
- Water-leak test photographs
- Underbody, suspension and brake photographs
- Part labels before removal
- Measurements before and after repair

The register should be updated whenever a public claim is confirmed, corrected
or rejected by vehicle evidence.

## In-depth resource map

There are substantially deeper resources than the summary pages used for the
initial dossier. They fall into four layers:

### Factory/service information

- **Mercedes-Benz / smart WIS and ASRA**: repair procedures, component
  locations, wiring references, specifications, maintenance operations and
  labour information. Access is normally subscription, dealer or professional
  workshop based. This is the preferred source for repair procedures.
- **XENTRY/DAS diagnostic system**: module diagnostics, measured values,
  actuation tests, teach-in/adaptation functions and coding. A generic OBD
  reader does not provide equivalent coverage.
- **Mercedes-Benz EPC / parts catalogue**: VIN- and option-dependent component
  groups, exploded views, part numbers and supersessions. Fitment must still be
  checked against the VIN and market.

These are the resources to use for exact torque values, connector procedures,
airbag work, actuator teach-in, coding and safety-critical repairs.

### Publicly indexed technical documents

The [Smart Car Manuals library](https://smartcarmanuals.com/manuals/) currently
indexes much deeper documents than ordinary owner pages, including:

- **1998–2015 Smart Technical & Service Reference**, listed as 977 pages and
  covering the 450/451 family with related powertrain information.
- **2007–2014 Smart Workshop Repair Manual**, listed as 4,602 pages and
  including the 452 among several chassis. It is a large cross-generation
  reference, so every procedure must be filtered for 452 applicability.
- **2003–2006 452 SAM Electrical Schematic (DE)**, listed as 14 pages and
  identified as OEM-origin wiring information.
- **2003–2006 452 SAM Wiring Schematic (RHD)**, listed as 1 page.
- **2003–2006 452 Water Penetration Remedial Measures Guide**, listed as 60
  pages and dedicated to the chassis.
- **Roadster 452 Operating Manual**, listed as 258 pages for owner operation,
  equipment and warnings.
- **1999–2005 Smart 450/452/454 Glasurit Paint Color Codes**, listed as 13
  pages.

Start with the individual document pages rather than assuming that a search
result or a downloaded filename is authentic:

- [452 SAM Electrical Schematic (DE)](https://smartcarmanuals.com/manuals/2003-2006-smart-roadster-452-sam-electrical-schematic-de/)
- [452 SAM Wiring Schematic (RHD)](https://smartcarmanuals.com/manuals/2003-2006-smart-roadster-452-sam-wiring-schematic-rhd/)
- [Smart manuals library](https://smartcarmanuals.com/manuals/)

The index labels several documents as OEM-origin, but hosting is not the same
as provenance. For safety-critical use, compare the document revision, market,
language and vehicle scope with a lawfully obtained factory source.

### Parts and community technical references

- Smart Car Manuals' [452 model index](https://smartcarmanuals.com/models/452-roadster/)
  provides a searchable starting point for parts, figures, fault-code guides
  and maintenance topics.
- Specialist Smart forums and enthusiast guides can provide discontinued-part
  substitutions, failure symptoms and owner-validated repairs.
- Use these sources to generate investigation leads; promote a part number or
  modification to the project database only after VIN, dimensions and source
  evidence agree.

### What the current stack does not yet contain

The project currently contains summaries and source links, not a local mirror
of the large manuals. The next technically useful step is to work from a
lawfully obtained manual set and extract only project-relevant metadata:

- 452-specific system/component index
- Fuse and relay allocation
- Connector/module names and locations
- Fault-code index and diagnostic preconditions
- Fluid and capacity table
- Torque/specification table
- Service operations and prerequisites
- Parts group/diagram references
- Water-ingress repair locations

That extraction can make the project much more detailed without copying entire
manuals or redistributing copyrighted pages.
